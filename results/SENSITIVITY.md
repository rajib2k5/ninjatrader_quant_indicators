# Sensitivity of the P1 ranking to the daily close and to costs

> Educational research only. Not financial advice. See DISCLAIMER.md.

Variants (D-frame close only; Open/High/Low/Volume and the 15m frame are the same in all three,
so 15m rules have identical numbers):

- **v1** = `results/v1_last_trade/` - 16:00 last trade (v1, pre-registered section 3)
- **A1** = `results/` - settlement close (A1, PROTOCOL section 9; the ranking)
- **15:00** = `results/close_1500/` - 15:00 CT one-minute close

SR = annualised Sharpe of daily net P&L over 2021-01-04 -> 2026-02-24. Rank at 0x / 2x = the rank in that cost run (section 8 tie rule inside the run). H1 / H2 ranks order the A1 rules by the half's SR (ties < 0.005 broken A->Z; no DSR per half).
